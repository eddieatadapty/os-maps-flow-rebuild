# OS Maps Growth Audit

**Live → https://eddieatadapty.github.io/os-maps-flow-rebuild/**

Five ideas to turn more OS Maps users into subscribers, each built into a clickable version of OS Maps's own onboarding. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Ask your three questions before the paywall, then answer them | +10–20% CR to purchase |
| 02 | Ask for the account after the trial starts | +5–10% CR to purchase · ARPU up to +17% |
| 03 | Put 101,232 ratings where people decide | +10–15% CR to purchase |
| 04 | Make £3 a month impossible to miss | +10–15% ARPU (more annual plans) |
| 05 | Catch the people who tap Skip | +5–10% CR from people who close the paywall |

Every price, rating and feature comes from OS Maps's App Store listing, website or in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty’s expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across 30,000+ subscription apps, not measured lift for OS Maps. They overlap, so they don’t add up.

Static single page, no build step: `python3 -m http.server`.
